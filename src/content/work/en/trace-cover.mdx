## OBSERVATION

Long-document retrieval systems can return relevant evidence while still missing coverage-critical parts of the source material.

## THE BET

A benchmark should ask not only whether retrieved evidence is relevant, but whether the system can prove what evidence path it covered.

## WHAT I BUILT

The current work is a research harness for requirement-evidence traceability in long multimodal documents, with a focus on low-cost model collaboration and coverage evaluation.
