## Background

AI made generation cheap. Pages, tools, assets, and experiments can be produced quickly, but deciding what is worth doing and what is worth continuing becomes harder.

## The idea

Vulcan is aimed at that selection problem: put opportunity discovery, asset production, and result tracking into one flow so the system helps decide what is worth continuing, not only what can be generated.

## What I built

It includes canonical opportunities, tool engines, and an agent build process for turning detected demand into product surfaces. The internal “signal to output” flow is less important than connecting discovery, production, and review.
