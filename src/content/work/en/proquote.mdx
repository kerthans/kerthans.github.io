## OBSERVATION

In managed IT services, every conversation eventually reaches a proposal. The proposal is not only a sales artifact. It is the first operational surface where scope, pricing, recurring revenue and trust become visible.

The opportunity was to treat quoting as a system surface, not a formatted document.

The gap was architectural.

## THE BET

A proposal tool built around the full quote-to-acceptance arc.

The system handles quote construction, MRR calculation, one-time project scoping, template management, proposal layout, online acceptance, signature capture, win-data tracking and PDF export.

The bet: if the quote and proposal share one commercial structure, the system can shorten the distance between service work and a signed decision.

## WHAT I BUILT

A quote editor. A proposal surface. An acceptance path.

The system had three layers. **Construction.** A quote editor that understood MRR versus one-time work. Templates encoded pricing logic, not just layout. **Presentation.** A proposal renderer turned the same commercial structure into a client-ready document. **Acceptance.** Online acceptance and signature capture gave the proposal a direct decision path, with PDF export as a fallback surface.

The important decision was shared structure: the editor and proposal surface render the same commercial model.

## THE SYSTEM LEFT TRACES

The product exists as a live system.

ProQuote produces quotes, generates proposals, supports online acceptance and preserves PDF output. Once the system shipped, the constraint moved from construction to distribution.

The trace is technical and strategic: a working quote-to-proposal pipeline, and a sharper question about how proposals actually enter MSP buying conversations.

## THEN REALITY ANSWERED

The system changed the shape of the question.

After shipping, implementation stopped being the primary uncertainty. The next question became exposure: who needs this first, what workflow it enters, and whether the proposal is the wedge or the destination product.

Reality moved the investigation outward.

## THE DECISION

CONTINUE

The product system is live. The next experiment is distribution.

Keep ProQuote operational. Do not add a larger feature layer until the channel is better understood.

## WHAT REMAINED

Some uncertainty cannot be reduced through better architecture.

ProQuote sits in a live state. It can produce a quote, render a proposal and capture a signature. The next phase tests whether those capabilities meet a real distribution curve.

Either outcome is signal.
